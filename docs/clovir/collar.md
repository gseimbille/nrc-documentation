The collar carries the electronics, the battery, and the generator that produces
the warning sound and the electrical stimulation. Around that housing sit fabric
elements protecting the animal's neck, plus a strap and counterweights.

![The collar worn by an animal](/img/clovir/collar-on-animal.jpg)

*Version 4 collar, worn in the BOTTOM position.*

## Components

![Collar components laid out](/img/clovir/collar-components.jpg)

From top to bottom: the protective sleeve, the housing holding the electronics
and battery inside its padded support, the counterweight, and the strap.

## Powering on and off

The collar has no button. It is switched using a **magnet**, held against the
marked area of the housing:

| Action | Hold the magnet for |
| --- | --- |
| Switch **off** | 5 seconds |
| Switch **on** | 10 seconds |

![Where to apply the magnet](/img/clovir/collar-power-on.jpg)

## Fitting it on the animal

The collar can be worn in either position:

- **TOP** — on top of the animal's neck.
- **BOTTOM** — underneath the neck, as in the photo above. The counterweights hold
  it in place.

Videos showing how to assemble the components for each position are available
from NRC.

:::tip[Check it reports before releasing the animal]
After switching a collar on, confirm it appears **online** and **in zone** before
letting the animal into the perimeter — see
[Operational process](./operational-process.md).
:::
